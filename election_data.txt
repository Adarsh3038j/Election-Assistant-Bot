IMPORTANT ELECTION INFORMATION:
1. Agle local elections 01 May 2006 ko honge.
2. Voter registration ki aakhri tareekh 01 May 2006 hai.
3. Naya voter ID banwane ke liye "Form 6" bharna padta hai. 
4. Agar aapka address change hua hai toh "Form 8" bharna hoga.
5. Voting ke din Polling station subah 7:00 AM se shaam 6:00 PM tak khule rahenge.
6. Online voting allowed nahi hai, sabko EVM ke through physical polling booth par ja kar vote dena hoga.
